# Idea

So my idea is basically:

- you’re a poor fucker on Scrapyard and one of the peeps tasked with scavenging 
- you own an Ox’vic Pyrr Vehicle that has a hydrogen engine with hydrogen fuel cells
- you drive around a desert landscape with ship wrecks scattered around that you can go to and find supplies like food, hydrogen power cells, food printers, materials, scraps, etc that you all put in the car using physics interactions and stuff like that (you have to physically put the stuff in the trunk i guess, see repo for an example)
- there are enemy scavengers that you can run into that will try to kill you (you use either fps gameplay to shoot the others down)
- you drive back to your tribe and leave the stuff you found there and you can talk to npcs and stuff 
- ships sometimes fly over? 
- sandstorms, weather!! 
- vehicle damage and repair
	- repair tool?
	- tyre replacement?
