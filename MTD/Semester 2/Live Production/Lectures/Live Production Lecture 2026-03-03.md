#### LIVE PRODUCTION

###### TEAM

half-exercise group, approximately 10 students  
coordinated by administration, look for specific groups in [LEVIS](https://levis.fh-ooe.at/)

Roles needed within one production team:

- Director (operates Video Switching System, head of production)
- Assistent Director (assists in operation, prepares external material, graphic overlays etc.)
- Camera Operator x2 (Setup + Live camera)
- Sound Engineer (prepares and mixes all audio sources: mics, laptops, external players etc.)
- Light Engineer (design and operates studio lighting)
- Moderation, "Guests" (preparation and presentation of content on stage, stage hands during setup)  

    Exact number of personnel differ, depending on planned production 

###### TOPIC

Is to be decided by production team (educational content, fictional show, product presentation, personal interest (e.g. game / film review) etc.) 

###### REQUIREMENTS

Production has to be prepared including:

- cue sheet / production plan ("Regieplan")
- unique content including presenter & guests (in studio, or remote per live-call)
- dedicated graphic style for intro (anim. or video) / inserts / overlays / end-credits
- suitable stage / set design including lights
- remote content (prerecorded/produced material that is played back during live show)
