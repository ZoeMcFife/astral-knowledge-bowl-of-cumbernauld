Outline for Exercise Project _**Projectvideo**_

- Production of a **max. three minute long video** that showcases your _semesterproject_ in the style of a **corporate / promotional video**
- **Conception & planning** as well as **editing & postproduction in ~5 person team** (quarter exercise group)
- **Studio shoot** of _talking head_ (person presenting / talking about the project) setting **in ~10 person team** (quarter exercise group)
- exact team size depends on total number of semesterprojects
    - **every semesterproject has** to have **an assigned video-production group**
    - **no one is allowed to produce the video for their project themselves** (industry like "external" production team ;)
- The material should be self-recorded content. stock footage/music (if needed) has to be license free and publishable on video platforms like YouTube.  
    - The video **_must_ include** a **self-shot _"talking head"_** (portrait/presentation/interview situation) with at least one person, **filmed in various shot sizes, well-lit, and with original sound**.
    - the latter will be filmed with support from video- and audio lecturers as part of a shared exercise of the two courses (AUP/VIP), e.g., at the Hagenberger LRS, or a campus-nearby location of your choice.
