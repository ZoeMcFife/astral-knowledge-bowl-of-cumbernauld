## what are some of the space games you’ve played recently?

Star Citzen, Elite, Oddity

Faulcon Delacy Cobra MK III - Federal Corvette

Aegis Avenger Titan

## What was your first reaction to using the interface of those ships?

Avenger Titan cockpit → gleamed as old and outdated, but sturdy and reliable → “trusty piece of shit”

Vulture → cut corners, rough edges, “works, whatever”

Connie → old fashioned but classy

====

usage…

Connie → leaves room for the window, buttons and usage are hard to find at first but easy once you know them

## what did you like about the ships ui?

the ui in sc is terrible 

the updated looks of the self status display give a good indication of what needs attentions (gradients of the hologram of the damage interface)

clunky, but tells you most of the things you do need to know 
## what did you find annoying?

- missile lock ui is not clear - doesn’t display information well 
- radar is clunky and unintuitive, hard to tell where a target it, radar distance setting doens’t help 
- 2D radars are mostly unusable
- engineering system is great, but backup panel is not good enough, not enough information, no power information, etc
- Collision warning system triggers too late. 
- visual representation of other players and stuff, block important ui during calls and such
- cannot tell if damage is piercing or not 
- a lot of other things that cannot be discussed due to time constraints
## what would you like to see in a ships ui?

- the dot thing from elite 
- quick navigation using keyboard or nub of a joystick
	- lists etc
- for combat: scanning hostile target successfully → weapon loadout information
	- do i have resistance to this enemy?
	- comparison between your and enemy ship
	- strengths and weaknesses
- task list

## Oddity specific

### likes

- familiar and easy to grasp
- grabs your attention
- things look how you’d expect them to look
- the altimeter, horizon, very intuitive 

### dislikes

- difficult to read due to resolution
- indication of shield recharge status unclear 
- heat ui should change color based on status
	- turn redder if it gets warmer
