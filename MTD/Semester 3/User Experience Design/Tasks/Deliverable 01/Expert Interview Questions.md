#user_experience_design 

Interviewee: Kathryn

## Q1 — What experience do you have with space sims and especially piloting ships?

I have a quite a lot of experience in space sims and flying ships, in games like star citizen, elite dangerous, no man’s sky.
## Q2 — What do you think is important for a ships UI?

The most important that comes to a ship UI is the communication to the player. It has to be clear and easy to read. You need to know what’s happening, quickly and easily, because you don’t always have the time to read. 

Ability to change colors (for accessibility purposes).
## Q3 — What information should be always available at a glance?

- Movement indication - would be unfortunate if you didn’t know you were moving and you are now miles away, Altimeter etc
- Ship status - what is the ship currently doing? How is it holding up?
- Fuel and Supply status, always viewable
##  Q4 — How dense should the UI be? How much viewable at the same time?

Depends on the activity.

- open space - more clear
- dogfight - extra information for that purposes for elxampe

essentials, but as low as possible - so you can still the surroundings
##  Q5 — How should information be prioritized in different scenarios?
