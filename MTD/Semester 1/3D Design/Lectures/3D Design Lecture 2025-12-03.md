# Path vs Ray Tracing

## Ray Tracing

- simulates light rays from camera
- calculates deterministacally
- no natural indirect light
- needs extra tracks for GI
- gets used in real time engines and older renderers 

## Path Tracing

- stochastic
- all rays create randon paths → reflection, diffusion, etc
- light bounces more times → real GI
- very realistic 
- many samples
