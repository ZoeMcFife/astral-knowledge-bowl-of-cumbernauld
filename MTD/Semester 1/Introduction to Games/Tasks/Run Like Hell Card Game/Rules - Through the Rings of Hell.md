# ⚔️ Game Rules

---

## 🎴 Setup
1. Shuffle all **Event Cards** and place them face down on the board.  
2. Shuffle all **Item Cards** and place them in a stack.  
3. Each player **chooses a Class Card**.  
4. Each player draws **3 random Item Cards** from the stack.  
5. Players start at the **START node**.

---

## 🧙 Classes and Stats

Each player selects a **Class Card** with unique stats and abilities.

### Attack
- Roll the corresponding number of **D6** as your attack roll.  
- **Damage** to an enemy is represented as **steps** (movement reduction).

### Carry Capacity
- Add up the **total weight** of your items.  
- If total weight **exceeds carry capacity**, reduce movement by the number of points **above capacity**.

### Speed
- Roll the corresponding number of **D6** for **steps** per turn.

### Special Ability
- Refer to the **class card description** for special effects.

---

## 🚶 Turn Order

Turn order is determined by **Speed** — fastest class starts first:

1. 🐀 Goblin  
2. 🐾 Thief  
3. 🔮 Mage  
4. ⚔️ Warrior  
5. ⛏️ Dwarf  

> You can only move **forward** — no turning back!

---

## 😈 The Devil

- After **Turn 3**, the **Devil** enters **Layer 7**.  
- After **another 2 turns**, the Devil reaches **Layer 6**.  
- Then **1 turns later**, the Devil reaches **Layer 5**.  
- Then **1 turn later**, the Devil reaches **Layer 4**.  
- The Devil **moves up one layer every turn** thereafter.  
- The Devil **always moves after all players** have taken their turns.

### If a Player is Caught
- Move the player up to the **nearest node** on the **layer above**.  
- Player **loses 5 random cards**.

---

## 🗺️ Event Cards

### Types
- **Landscape**
- **Trap**
- **Item**
- **Enemy**

### Rules
- Event Cards are **placed on each level**, face down.  
- When a player enters a space with an Event Card → **flip it over**.  
- Possible results:
  - 🧟 Fight an **Enemy** (Attack roll)
  - 🏃 Walk more steps (Speed roll)
  - 💰 Gain items (pull Item Card from stack)
- While standing on an Event card, you count to the lower field. Once the card has been defeated, you now count to the upper field.

Some events will instruct you to **pull additional Item Cards** as rewards.

---

## 🎒 Item Cards

- Item Cards are kept in a **stack** beside the board.  
- Items can be **pulled from certain events** or rewards.  
- Each player starts with **2 random Item Cards**.  

### Item Attributes
- **Weight** → affects speed and movement.  
- **Value** → used to determine victory.  
- **Special Traits** → some can target players or enemies. (Throwable items can only be thrown at players in the same field)

### Losing Items
- If a player **loses items** during an event:  
  - The **next player** may **randomly draw one item** from that player's inventory.

---

## ⚔️ Combat Rules

- During battle, players **lose 1 card per turn**.  
- **Warrior** is unaffected (keeps all cards).  
- **Fleeing** a battle costs **3 cards**.  
  - **Goblin** only loses **1 card** when fleeing.  

---

## 🧭 Movement Rules

- During a turn, a player can **walk up until the next Event Card**.  
- Only **one action per turn** (excluding walking).  
  - Example: Thief can only **steal once per turn**.  
- Minimum Movement is 1 per dice! regardless of weight.
	- e.g Gobling has minimum speed of 4

---

## 🏆 Victory Conditions

- The player with the **highest total item value** at the end **wins** the game.  

---

### ☠️ Summary of Flow

1. Players choose classes and draw 2 items.  
2. Determine turn order (by Speed).  
3. Players move, encounter events, and collect items.  
4. The Devil advances up layers as turns progress.  
5. When all players are caught or the board is cleared — tally **total value**.  
6. The player with the **most valuable hoard** is crowned **victor**!
