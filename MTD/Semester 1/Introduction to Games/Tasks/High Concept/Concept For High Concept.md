- The Milky Collective votes for John Lennon as the new Great Senator
    

- He is married to Ludwig Stevil
    

- He promises to raise taxes by 1%
    
- He gets murdered in cold blood (who could it be :O :O )
    
- The Milky Collective hires Mr. Mister, the galaxies best detective and his partner in solving crime: Miss Puppy. 
    
- They have their own spaceship! The Amor Historicus!!!!
    
- Their first task is to investigate the crime scene of Johns murder
    
- They find a bullet casing from a very specific gun with an a manufacturer engraving -> that’s where they head next (its a small gun manugactuer)
    
- It’s on a planet called Barrakis (Dune Reference) -> they interrogate the gun shop guy and that leads to “coffee shop at the end of the universe” (don’t they need an infinite improbability drive to reach it smh)=
    
- Im dead. There are like 8 locations that they can visit -> each with their own clues and puzzles to solve and choices to be made. Imagine like each planet has clues and you can take multiple paths to reach the conclusion. Someone smart with lore and worldbuilding and storytelling could make an interesting story with reasons but im too tired to do that right so i’m keeping it vague, anyways
    
- They finally reach their conclusion: the murderer was no one other than john lennons boy wife ludwig stevil!! His last known position is a vacation planet called richtopolis -> they go and board his space yacht for questioning. Then ludwig stevil twists his evil moustaché and wants to kick them out, but oh ho ho ho no. Miss Puppy doesn’t like that and demands answers! (she throws the proof on the table) Ludwig gets mad… so so mad… and then they have a very cinematic fire fight and shit. 
    
- Mr. mister manages to take ludwig out without killing him. Victory
