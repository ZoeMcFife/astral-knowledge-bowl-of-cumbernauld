Mina is smart, creative and really talented when working on spaceships and other technology. Her dream has always been to become an adventurer and go out on quests and adventures out in space and visit new star systems, but her low self-esteem and social anxiety has kept her anchored on the Ising Interstellar Spaceport, working as a mechanic. Despite longing for change, she struggles to believe she deserves more than the life she already has. She wishes for change every single day, but lacks the motivation to actually change something.
## Early Life

She was born in 2996, on 109 Piscium 3, a large icy planet, in a small settlement. Growing up in gravity, lead her to develop regular muscles and bones. After her parents landed jobs on the Ising Interstellar Spaceport, the entire family relocated to the station. 

When Mina was around 12 years old, her brother got killed in a mining accident, caused by the unsafe working conditions of the Piscium Mining Corporation. Her parents sued the company and won a significant settlement. While the money helped them out a lot, the trauma of losing their son still lingers on. Mina’s father sank into depression, becoming bitter and distant. 

Around Minas 15th Birthday, her parents got her an old multipurpose ship, that was first used in 2750. They wanted her to use it to live her life to the fullest, but it mostly sits docked to the spaceport, only getting used for satellite repair trips. 

Mina named the ship “The Tortellini”.

## Current Life

Mina currently lives on the Ising Interstellar Spaceport, a large industrial mining facility. There she works as a mechanic, usually repairing mining ships or old satellites in the star system. It’s been like this for the past couple of years. 

On a regular Tuesday in 3016, Mina flies out to fix some old satellites, but this time it’s a bit different. She gets attacked by multiple small, unmarked ships. Mina manages to fight them off, but she thinks it’s really weird, she did not recognize any of the ships. They were definitely not cat-made ships. 

She reports the incident and hands in sensor data from her ship, but her case doesn’t get prioritized and lands on the backburner. Mina is really pissed about that. 

On the same day, the company she works for assigns her a coworker: Alyx Maria Spirit. She’s supposed to assist Mina with repairs, but Mina doesn’t really need help and Alyx isn’t particularly interested in the job either.
